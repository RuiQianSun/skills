Definitions and how the sample data was made
Session
One visit to the site, counted on the day it started.
Signup
A visit that ended with a new account.
Conversion rate
Signups divided by sessions over the same days.
Revenue
First-month revenue from those signups, in US dollars.
Previous period
The same number of days just before the window. A window that starts too early to have one shows no change.
Sample data
Every number is synthetic, from a script you can read in Sources. It builds in a Paid ads budget cut on 1 March 2026, a localized APAC launch on 15 June 2026, a one-day outage on 9 July 2026, monthly Email sends on the first Tuesday, and late-November holiday peaks.
