Definitions and how the sample data was made
Sell-through
Units sold divided by units received, over the 12 weeks so far.
Recommended, kept
The assortment engine picked the SKU for that store and the planner kept it.
Added by planner
The engine did not pick the SKU for that store; a planner added it.
Dropped by planner
The engine picked it and a planner removed it, so the store never received it.
Ran out of stock
A store-SKU had a week where demand was higher than what was left on the shelf.
Units of demand missed
An estimate of what would have sold in those weeks with stock on hand.

Every number is synthetic, from a script you can read in Sources: 40 stores in 4 clusters, 150 SKUs in 5 categories, one buy per store-SKU at the start of the season and no replenishment.
