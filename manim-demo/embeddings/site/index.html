Word and text embeddings, retrieval

Embedding Explorer

A language model turns each word or passage into 384 numbers. Things with similar meaning get similar numbers, which is what search and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) are built on. Everything below uses real vectors from the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model.

The projector

240 words and 40 short passages, squeezed from 384 dimensions down to 3 so you can see them. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a point to see its nearest neighbors. Neighbors are measured in the full 384 dimensions, so they are more trustworthy than how close points look.
