# Critic review: predictive and prescriptive drafts (one critic for both stages)

- F1 (forecast MPG within 1.5 mpg, twice as good as class median): horizon leaks: carried-over configurations are memorised, and "train on all years before Y" assumes last year's ratings are complete when acting. The class-median baseline is a straw man. Revised: score only configurations whose spec was never seen before, train on years up to Y-2 (a one-year gap), baseline = median of earlier configurations with the same class, cylinders, turbo, hybrid and drive. EVs are already excluded from the data.
- P1 (flag predicted 30+ mpg vs hybrids-only, on recall): no objective or payoff; the comparison was recall only; the status quo was too weak. Revised: stated payoff (+1 per correct target, -1 per wrong target, 0 per miss), compared with two status-quo rules, hybrids only and "hybrid, or a car of 2.0 L or less with no turbo", on new configurations with the same one-year gap. Configurations are not sales; the paper says so.
