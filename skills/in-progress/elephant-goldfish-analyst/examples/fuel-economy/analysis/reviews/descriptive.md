# Critic review: descriptive stage (drafts before revision)

- D1 (turbo share slope >= 1 pp/yr): the decision depends on the level of turbo adoption among small engines, not the slope; one fitted slope hides plateaus. Revised: among non-hybrid configurations of 2.0 L or less, turbo share >= 50% in every model year.
- D2 (30+ mpg under 20% of each year): "stay a niche" implies no rising trend; configurations are not sales. Revised: under 20% every year and fitted slope <= 1 pp/yr; the paper states the configurations-not-sales caveat.
- D3 (FWD at least 4 mpg over AWD, raw medians): mostly vehicle mix (class, engine size), not a drive effect. Revised: within the same class and engine-size band (0.5 L), non-hybrids only, median FWD-minus-AWD gap >= 2 mpg every year.
