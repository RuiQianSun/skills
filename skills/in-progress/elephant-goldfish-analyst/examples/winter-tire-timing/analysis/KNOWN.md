# What practitioners already know (Canadian winter-tire changeover)

- The industry rule of thumb: switch to winter tires once temperatures stay below 7 C; switch back above 7 C in spring.
- Fall demand peaks in October and November and spring demand in April and May.
- Quebec mandates winter tires from December 1 to March 15, so its fall rush is pulled by the law as well as the weather.
- The Prairies get cold and snow earlier than the coasts; Vancouver rarely needs winter tires.
- A first snowfall triggers a rush of last-minute changeovers.
