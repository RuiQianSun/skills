# What practitioners already know (Canadian tire retail)

Drafted by the elephant from general industry knowledge; the user should correct and extend it.

- Sales peak at the fall winter-tire changeover (October to November) and again in spring (April to May); January and February are the slowest months.
- The changeover is weather-driven: first snow comes earlier inland and on the Prairies than on the coasts.
- Larger-population provinces have larger sales; shares move slowly year to year.
- Same-month-last-year is the standard naive baseline for a seasonal retail forecast.
- Unadjusted monthly totals favour 31-day months (October has 31 days, November 30).
